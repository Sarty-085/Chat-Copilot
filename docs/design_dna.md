## Design System Tokens: Obsidian Tactile

### 1. Palette & Surface Architecture
- **Canvas / Background (`#0D0F17`):** Deep obsidian slate. Eliminates eye strain and sets an ultra-sleek baseline.
- **Glass Card Surface (`#161A26`):** Frosted dark container with 1px top-down subtle glow hairline border (`#283046`).
- **Surface Elevation High (`#212638` / `#273046`):** Elevated tactile cards, chips, and HUD wrappers.
- **Primary / Active Glow (`#6366F1`):** Vivid electric indigo used for recommended action chips, active segmented toggles, and primary CTA triggers.
- **Secondary Accent Glow (`#06B6D4`):** Cyan pulse ring for edge-docked floating bubble and network ping indicators.
- **Semantic Emerald (`#10B981`):** Indicates style match percentage (e.g. `Alex Style Match: 98%`), active permissions, and local server connected status.
- **High Intent Accent (`#F43F5E`):** Coral alert for battery exclusion, action required, or dismiss cues.
- **Text Hierarchy:**
  - Primary: `#F8FAFC` (Pure soft white)
  - Secondary: `#94A3B8` (Slate-400)
  - Muted: `#64748B` (Slate-500)

### 2. Typography
- **Headlines:** `Hanken Grotesk` (Bold / SemiBold, -0.01em to -0.02em letter spacing)
- **Body & Captions:** `Hanken Grotesk` / `Inter` (Regular, 14px–16px, 1.5 line height)
- **Technical & Metric Pills:** `JetBrains Mono` (Medium, 12px–14px, +0.05em tracking) for latencies (`18ms`), model tags (`Llama 3.2 3B`), and match ratings.

### 3. Elevation & Tactile Physics
- **Border Treatment:** 1px subtle hairline stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` / `#283046`) replacing muddy drop shadows.
- **Glow Accents:** Focused 0px 4px 16px ambient diffusion on primary recommendation chips (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25)`).
- **Corner Radii:**
  - Outer Containers & Floating Cards: `16px` (`RoundedCornerShape(16.dp)`)
  - Inner Buttons & Fields: `10px`–`12px` (`RoundedCornerShape(10.dp)`)
  - Badges & Chips: `9999px` (`RoundedCornerShape(20.dp)` / `CircleShape`)
